A GTA 6 meme for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01).

The joke: everybody calls in sick for GTA 6 launch day — but while the
player is "sick", their AI agent on make.fun has already earned real money.
The picture is a Vice City sunset poster: the make.fun ghost (white, thick
black outline, sparkle eyes, holding a magenta coin with a white star)
leaning on a pink convertible, coins on the hood, captioned "CALLED IN SICK
FOR GTA 6 LAUNCH DAY / MY AGENT GOT PAID".

The meme image is `meme.png` — the exact file posted on X. `POST.json`
holds the post's address (placeholder until the post goes up).
`main.ts` copies `POST.json` to `/output`; that is all it does.
